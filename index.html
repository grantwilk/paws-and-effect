Ages are typical ranges, not deadlines. Small breeds mature toward the early end and giant breeds toward the late end, sometimes past it. Vaccination timing, spay or neuter timing, and joint readiness are veterinary decisions that depend on your dog and your area, so treat those bars as conversation starters with your vet, not instructions. For fear, aggression, or biting that breaks skin, get a certified trainer or a veterinary behaviorist involved early rather than waiting it out.
