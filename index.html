What the bars and symbols mean
Solid end cap — this window closes for good. You cannot open it again.
Striped — not yet. Wait until the bar ends.
Faded right edge — this continues after two years.
Pale — continue this, but it is no longer the main job.
Diamond — one event, not a period of time.

These ages are typical ranges, not deadlines. Small breeds mature near the early end of each range, and giant breeds near the late end. Vaccination dates, spay or neuter dates, and joint readiness are veterinary decisions. They depend on the dog and on your area. Use those bars to start a conversation with your vet, not as instructions. If the dog shows fear or aggression, or bites hard enough to break skin, get a certified trainer or a veterinary behaviorist early.
